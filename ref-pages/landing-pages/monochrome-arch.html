Listen first

Every project begins with weeks of observation. We study how light moves through the site before we draw a single line.

Subtract

We add nothing decorative. Materials speak when left honest. Concrete ages. Wood darkens. Copper turns green. These are features.

Time as material

Buildings should improve with decades. We specify materials that gain character rather than degrade. Maintenance is part of the design.

Start a
conversation

We take on three to four projects per year. If you are considering building something that will last, we would like to hear about it.
