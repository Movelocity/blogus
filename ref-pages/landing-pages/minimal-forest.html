Less, but chosen well

We work with twelve makers who share one conviction: the things you live with daily should be made with the same care as the things you display. No decoration for its own sake. No trend cycles. Just honest materials shaped with patience.

Stoneware

Fired at 1280 degrees in a wood kiln in Shigaraki. Each piece carries the atmosphere of its firing.

White oak

Air-dried for eighteen months before carving. The grain stays visible. No lacquer, only beeswax.

European flax

Stone-washed once. Softens further with every use. Gets better after a hundred washes, not worse.
